Questions

Which Navisworks versions does it work with?

Navisworks Manage 2026 and 2027 on Windows. The installer adds the IZA tab to both if you have both.

Does it change my model?

Only when you click a button that creates or deletes something, and it shows what it will do first. Ctrl+Z undoes it. Your RVT and NWC files are never touched.

Does it need the internet?

No. The licence is checked on your PC and no model or usage data is sent anywhere.

What does "12 months of updates" mean?

You get every new version released in the 12 months after you buy. After that the version you have keeps working with no time limit; renew for RM 59 if you want newer versions.

I changed my PC. What now?

Send your new computer ID by email or WhatsApp. A licence can be moved to a new PC once a year at no charge.

Can I get a refund?

Please use the 14-day trial to make sure it fits your work. Licences are not refundable once the key is issued; a key made for the wrong computer ID is replaced free.

Can my company get a quotation and invoice?

Yes. Team and Company licences are invoiced under our registered business. Email the company name, address and number of PCs.
